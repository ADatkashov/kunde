# 08 — Service Category UX Patterns

All category screens share identity, location, listing details, price transparency, support and status components. Category-specific flows below remain distinct; category availability is controlled by operational readiness. Every price clearly says fixed, from, estimate, quote or pending confirmation.

| Category | Landing/catalog/search | Detail and selection | Transaction, status, cancellation/review |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mobility** | Map/list entry with pickup, destination and service type; saved/recent endpoints only with consent; tier filters | Fare estimate and expiry, vehicle/service attributes, accessibility options where real, pickup pin with address alternative | Review pickup/dropoff/tier/estimate before request; dispatch and provider assignment shown as separate; live tracking only from verified feed; no-provider timeout, route changes and cancel policy explicit; rate after eligible completed trip |
| **Food & Shopping** | Merchant tabs/categories, item search, open/closed and real delivery/pickup eligibility; dietary filters only from verified catalog | Menu/product detail with options, ingredients/allergens only when supplied, stock freshness, modifiers and quantity | Merchant-scoped cart; address/pickup; itemized quote; substitutions require explicit preference; accepted/preparing/ready/picked up/in transit/delivered; missing/incorrect line item support and line-level refund state; review after completion |
| **Home** | Service type, area, fixed-price vs quote filter, date availability | Scope inclusions/exclusions, provider credentials where verified, estimate basis, access/material requirements | Request assessment; present final quote and scope, require acceptance before work; booking/visit; in progress/completed evidence; cancellation/call-out fee disclosed; quality dispute and review |
| **Health** | Directory by specialty, location, in-person/online only where supported; show credential/clinic provenance | Clinician profile, specialty and clinic, available time, fee and cancellation; no diagnosis or claim not verified | Booking requires consent for necessary data; secure intake only if approved; reminders; cancellation/reschedule/no-show per reviewed policy; no sensitive health data in public review; category stays disabled pending compliance approval |
| **Personal Care** | Service type, location, date, staff and price filters | Service duration, employee profile, price, resource/availability, optional add-ons | Select service + staff/resource + slot; hold and confirm; calendar reminder, check-in/service completion; reschedule/cancel policy; provider complaint/review |
| **Family & Education** | Activities/classes by area, age band, schedule, capacity and accessibility | Instructor/organization, program, age eligibility, safety/credentials, fee, term and calendar | Guardian-authorized beneficiary; confirm schedule/payment; attendance/reminders; cancellation/withdrawal/refund under enrollment policy; communication routed through guardian; review after eligible attendance |
| **Experiences** | Events/venues by date, area, type, ticket availability | Organizer/venue, date/time zone, ticket class/seat, accessibility, age/entry terms, final fees | Select quantity/seats; inventory hold; payment then verified ticket issuance; digital pass stored; admission/scan status; event cancellation/refund or dispute shown; no resale/transfer unless explicitly supported |
| **Auto** | Vehicle-compatible service categories, location, appointment vs roadside | Service scope, compatible vehicle attributes, facility/provider, estimate or inspection-required quote, duration | Choose appointment or eligible roadside dispatch; additional work needs price/scope approval; status received/inspection/authorized/in service/ready/completed; cancellation/call-out rule, evidence-backed dispute and rating |

## Shared discover-to-commit pattern

Browse/filter → detail → choose category-specific fields → live validation/quote/slot/inventory → review exact beneficiary/location/time/price/terms → user confirmation → pending provider/payment state → verified completion or recovery. Do not show a generic cart for appointment/dispatch categories. Order and Booking may link but are not one state machine.

## Category readiness

Health, family/education, experience tickets and each other category remain gated by PRD 1.1 launch decisions. Hide or explain unavailable capabilities without presenting mock listings as live. If an external integration is absent, remove map/ticket/live dispatch promises and retain truthful list/manual fallback.
