# 12 — Marketplace Quality and Moderation

## Quality lifecycle

Pre-publication verification → catalog quality checks → active monitoring → complaint/report triage → proportionate action → partner/provider remediation → appeal → reactivation or termination. Listing eligibility and fulfillment readiness are separate checks. Quality score informs operations but must not be a hidden, unappealable legal decision.

## Standards and signals

- Partner/provider identity and category credentials are verified according to risk and law.
- Catalog has truthful price basis, availability, descriptions, service area, cancellation terms, images/right to use and prohibited-goods check.
- Customer complaints include fulfillment, safety, quality, misleading price, fraud, privacy and review abuse.
- Signals: acceptance, completion, cancellation by actor/reason, late rate, refund/dispute, repeat complaints, confirmed safety event, review manipulation. Normalize by volume and uncertainty; do not penalize a small partner solely for sparse data.

## Reviews and ratings

Only eligible completed transaction participants can review relevant business/service/provider, once per defined target/transaction. Clearly label verified transaction reviews; do not pay for positive rating or suppress critical feedback solely for sentiment. Remove/edit only for defined policy breach (personal data, threat, spam, conflict, irrelevant content, unlawful content) with reason and appeal. Partner response is allowed under conduct rules and does not erase review. Ranking uses rating + count/confidence and relevance; no artificial score inflation.

## Moderation outcomes

No action; request information; content edit/remove with reason; listing restricted; temporary suspension; permanent termination. Immediate temporary hold allowed for credible safety/legal/security risk; human review promptly follows. Adverse action includes evidence references, policy version, actor, scope, notice where safe, remediation and appeal route. Automated detection only prioritizes queues; fraud/quality model does not alone terminate a partner or deny consumer financial rights.

## Fraud and duplicate detection

Candidate signals: repeated accounts/payment attempts, unusual refund/chargeback patterns, business duplicates, referral abuse, review clusters, changed payout destination, device/IP anomalies. Apply privacy/minimization and human review. Avoid protected-class proxies and disclose only safe reason code. Store decision evidence securely and expire it under approved retention.
