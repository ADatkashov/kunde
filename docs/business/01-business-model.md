# 01 — Business Model

## Platform role

KUNDE is a consumer discovery and transaction coordination platform connecting local consumers with businesses and independent providers across service categories. A managed marketplace is the recommended starting point: KUNDE governs listing eligibility, discovery, transaction state, customer communications, support routing, platform policies and measurement. Category contracts determine whether a merchant, provider or KUNDE is the seller/service supplier, who fulfills, who is payee, and which party owns consumer obligations. Do not represent KUNDE as the seller, employer, carrier or payment intermediary unless counsel and contracts establish that role.

## Models by category

| Category | Recommended operating model | Candidate monetization | Operational caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Food & Shopping | Merchant marketplace; merchant prepares; delivery may be merchant, third-party or KUNDE-contracted | Commission; optional disclosed delivery/service fee | Store/product availability, acceptance time, missing-item remedies |
| Mobility | Dispatch/booking marketplace or contracted transport service | Commission/platform fee where lawful and contractually clear | Provider/vehicle eligibility, fare basis, safety, cancellation and licensing |
| Home | Lead/quote and booking marketplace; provider contracts scope/price with user | Success commission or transparent lead/business subscription | Quote vs estimate, service quality disputes, access and damage claims |
| Health | Directory/appointment discovery only until regulated model approved | Listing/business tools only if lawful; transaction commission requires review | Medical licensing, clinical independence, sensitive data and advertising restrictions |
| Personal Care | Appointment marketplace with partner service delivery | Booking/transaction commission; partner SaaS later | Staff capacity, deposits, no-show and rescheduling |
| Family & Education | Course/booking marketplace with guardian flow | Enrollment commission or business tools | Child safeguarding/privacy, refunds, class capacity |
| Experiences | Ticket/booking marketplace; organizer controls inventory/entry rules | Ticketing/booking fee if lawful and disclosed | Ticket issuance/validity, organizer liability, cancellation |
| Auto | Booking/quote marketplace; service center performs | Success commission, qualified lead or subscription | Inspection-dependent quote, parts, extra work and damage disputes |

These are candidate models, not final category legal characterizations.

## Value exchange

- **Consumer pays** the disclosed merchant/provider price and any clearly itemized fee/delivery/tax collected through the approved payment flow. Optional paid membership is deferred until quantified consumer value exists.
- **Partner pays** a contractually disclosed commission, lead fee, subscription or promoted placement only when that service is enabled and measured. Commission is not silently added to customer amount.
- **Provider earns** the contractually defined service/delivery amount, net of only disclosed and lawful deductions.
- **KUNDE provides** discovery, catalog/availability tools, transaction orchestration, notifications, support routing, marketplace integrity and reporting. Exact service level and liability boundaries are contracted.

## Revenue maturity

Phase 1: transaction commission is the core hypothesis on a narrow operational category. Optional delivery/service fees may be charged only when user-visible, cost-supported and contract/legal reviewed. Phase 2: partner tools/subscription and sponsored placement pilots if they do not reduce organic trust. Later: consumer membership, loyalty and B2B analytics only after product/financial/privacy case. Advertising cannot secretly influence organic rankings.

## Governance

Track GMV separately from recognized platform revenue and cash collected on behalf of others. A service/category cannot go live on an app screen alone: signed supply, supply verification, fulfillment, payments/settlement, support, refund/dispute process, analytics and incident owner must be ready (PRD 1.1 §01/21).
