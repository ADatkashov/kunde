# 19 — Kazakhstan City Expansion

## City readiness scorecard

Score each dimension 0–2 (0 absent, 1 partial/mitigation, 2 evidenced); proposed launch gate: no zero for critical dimensions and overall threshold set by leadership after pilot. Score is a planning tool, not an automated decision.

| Dimension | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Demand | Customer interviews, category searches/conversion test, repeat intent and competitor/service alternatives |
| Supply | Verified active partners and coverage by zone/day/time; capacity and signed terms |
| Fulfillment | Merchant/provider/3PL lane with test completion, delay and return procedures |
| Finance | Supported payment method, settlement beneficiary, reconciliation and refund drill |
| Support | Published coverage, trained language support, case/escalation staffing |
| Legal/privacy | City/country service classification, data flows, consumer terms, tax and permits reviewed |
| Product/localization | Address/geocoding quality, language, currency/time zone, category catalog and app QA |
| Economics | Unit-level contribution with realistic delivery/support/promo costs and CAC test |
| Trust/safety | Partner/provider verification, incident response, moderation and appeal path |

## Rollout sequence

Expand by partner density and serviceable zone, not city boundary alone. Establish one local operations lead and supply owner; run limited hours first if support/fulfillment are not 24/7. Launch the proven category before adding categories with different providers/terms. Reuse platform capabilities but localize service areas, rates, schedules, merchant cohort and support roster. Monitor city-specific cancellation, acceptance, delivery, refund, support and contribution metrics.

## Pause/rollback

Pause new customer acquisition or affected category when payment integrity, safety, legal readiness, support backlog or fulfillment failure exceeds pre-agreed threshold. Keep active transaction resolution and required financial reconciliation. Resume only after owner signs corrective action and closed-loop test.
