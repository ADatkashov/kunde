# KUNDE Business Model & Operations Specification 1.0

**Status:** business and operations recommendations/hypotheses for validation; not signed commercial terms, legal advice, or a forecast. **Market focus:** Almaty, Kazakhstan. **Product:** KUNDE — *Everyday, made easier.*

## Baseline and governance

This specification supplements [PRD 1.0](../product/PRD-1.0.md), [PRD 1.1](../product/PRD-1.1/README.md), [Technical Architecture 1.0](../architecture/README.md) and [Design System & UX/UI 1.0](../design/README.md). It preserves: phased category launch, one consumer-facing marketplace, distinct order/booking/payment states, explicit AI confirmation, role-scoped access and category-specific operations.

No actual partners, courier fleet, payment provider, commission contracts, conversion results, support staffing or market unit economics are confirmed. Financial ranges in 03 and scenario values in 17 are **illustrative assumptions for sensitivity analysis only**. Owners must validate them with partner interviews, provider contracts, finance, Kazakhstan counsel and a controlled pilot.

## Business model at a glance

Recommend a **managed marketplace**, initially earning transaction commission on selected categories, while the actual seller/provider and payment flow are defined per contract/category. Optional consumer/partner subscriptions, sponsored placements and business tools are later experiments, not launch assumptions. Fulfillment may be merchant-, provider-, partner-logistics- or third-party-managed; KUNDE must disclose who performs each service and who is responsible.

## Open decisions and approval gates

Category launch selection, seller/merchant-of-record model, payment service roles, settlement cadence, tax invoice/VAT treatment, commission ranges, delivery ownership, provider classification, refund policy, support hours and regulated-service boundaries remain unresolved. Any payment flow must use a lawful, appropriately authorized payment provider; the Payment and Payment Systems Law prohibits providing regulated payment services without the relevant authorization/registration, subject to legal interpretation and statutory exceptions ([official Adilet law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/Z1600000011)). Counsel and licensed payment specialists must validate the exact KUNDE model before launch.

## Change control

These documents use “required” for constraints from earlier specs; “recommendation” for operating design; “hypothesis” for unvalidated commercial/metric assumptions; “TBD” for a decision owner. Changes to funds flow, consumer terms, category availability, privacy or role authority require updates to relevant PRD and architecture records.
