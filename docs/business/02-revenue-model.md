# 02 — Revenue Model and Monetization

All income sources are optional proposals; commercial terms, accounting treatment, taxes and timing require finance/legal validation. GMV and pass-through funds are not KUNDE revenue.

| Revenue stream | Payer / value | Recognition trigger (proposal) | Constraints, UX and risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction commission | Partner/provider pays for completed marketplace transaction/discovery | Recognize only according to signed contract and accounting policy, generally when performance obligation is met | Category-specific base, refund adjustments, disclose partner terms; avoid unearned commission on failed/cancelled orders |
| Partner subscription | Business pays for defined catalog, staffing, analytics or workflow tools | Monthly service period under contract | Free onboarding/pilot may be appropriate; do not charge for unavailable features |
| Featured placement | Partner pays for labeled sponsored visibility | Campaign delivery under approved ad policy | Always label sponsored; preserve organic relevance and user trust; category/region targeting consent review |
| Delivery fee | Consumer or merchant pays for a specific fulfillment service | On completed/contractually qualifying delivery, net of refund policy | Itemize before confirmation; who receives/fulfills fee must be clear; avoid hidden fee changes |
| Service/booking fee | Consumer or partner pays for platform transaction operations | Contractual service delivery event | Disclose before confirmation; consumer-protection review; not automatically applied to all categories |
| Lead fee | Partner pays for a qualified lead where no transaction occurs | Contract-defined, verified lead delivered | Define duplicate/fraud/no-contact exclusions; do not sell sensitive leads without permission |
| Premium consumer features | Consumer subscription for clearly defined additional value | Subscription service period | Defer until willingness-to-pay research; no paywall for core safety/support/status |
| B2B tools/API | Partner/enterprise pays for operational integration, analytics or seats | Subscription/usage contract | Data controller/processor, SLA, export and security responsibilities explicit |
| Loyalty/financial products | Possible future margin or funded program | Not defined | Defer; financial products/lending/wallets are out of initial scope |

## Revenue recognition / cash-flow separation

Maintain three ledgers/concepts: (1) platform revenue earned under contract; (2) customer money attributable to partner/provider fulfillment; (3) funds in transit/payable/receivable pending settlement/refund. Payment processor fees, taxes, commission, discount funding and delivery costs are separate line items. Finance confirms principal/agent accounting, VAT, settlement, refund and gross-vs-net reporting before dashboard publication.

## User-experience safeguards

Sponsored inventory is labeled. Customer-facing fees are itemized at quote/review and cannot appear after confirmation without renewed consent. Partner subscriptions do not guarantee organic rank. Promotional credits show payer and eligibility internally; consumer terms show what applies and expiry. Metrics report net commission after refunds separately from gross commission booked.
