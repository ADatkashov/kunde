# 18 — Almaty Launch Strategy

## Recommended launch shape

Run a controlled, limited-zone pilot for **one operationally proven high-frequency category**, with food ordering as the leading candidate because PRD 1.0 identifies it and the transaction path is well-defined. Prefer merchant pickup/partner delivery first; add one contracted last-mile lane only after cost, status events, responsibility and support are proven. Grocery and mobility/courier are next candidates, not simultaneous commitments. Do not launch health, family/child, ticketing or broad home services before specialist operational/legal gates.

No specific district, partner or demand level is assumed. Choose pilot micro-zones only after merchant density, actual serviceability, courier availability, support coverage and delivery-time data are mapped.

## Audience/supply hypotheses

Potential first users: residents/workers in a dense serviceable pilot zone who value local food discovery and reliable fulfillment. Validate through interviews and small demand tests; do not assume demographic or neighborhood behavior. Supply acquisition: founder/partner team recruits a manageable cohort by zone/category, validates menu accuracy and response capacity, and signs standard commercial/operational terms. Provider acquisition starts only for an agreed fulfillment lane; no fleet/freelancer pool is assumed.

## Staged rollout

| Stage | Work | Go criteria (all proposed gates) |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Readiness | Partner cohort, terms, categories, payment flow, support, privacy/security, operational runbooks | Signed/verified supply; full order/refund/reconciliation dry runs; staffed owner; no unresolved critical legal/security issue |
| 1. Internal/closed test | Staff/test accounts, simulated then limited real transactions under approved terms | Order/payment state matches provider; partner acceptance, cancellation, refund and support paths work; no duplicate financial effects |
| 2. Limited pilot | Invited consumers in selected service zone; capped volume and hours | Fulfillment, complaint/refund, contribution inputs and partner response tracked; daily incident review; pause thresholds established |
| 3. Public launch | Marketing in serviceable zone and published hours/categories | Support staffing, payment reconciliation, supply coverage and incident response support expected load; P0 defects closed |
| 4. Expand category/zone | Add one zone/category at a time | Existing cohort stable; new partner readiness and unit economics demonstrated; legal/operational signoff |

## Marketing and acquisition

Start with partner-owned customer reach, opt-in referral, local content and measured digital campaigns. Promotions are capped and attributed to funder; no discount-first strategy until repeat/contribution is known. Use channel cohorts to estimate CAC and fraud. Avoid paid acquisition beyond support/supply capacity. Public claims match actual available hours/areas and ETA confidence.

## Launch owners and controls

Named owners required: market lead, partner operations, fulfillment/logistics, customer support, finance/reconciliation, trust/compliance, product/engineering on-call. Daily pilot review covers failed/late transactions, unknown payments, refunds, support aging, unsafe events and supply gaps. Kill switch can disable category/zone; active orders retain customer support and financial reconciliation.
