# 23 — Legal and Compliance Considerations

This is a **legal issue checklist, not legal advice or a legal opinion**. Kazakhstan law and implementing rules change; obtain current advice from Kazakh counsel, tax adviser, licensed payment specialist and relevant category experts before contracting or launch.

## Required professional review

| Topic | Questions for counsel/specialist |
|---|---|
| Marketplace role | Is KUNDE intermediary, agent, information platform, seller, service provider or mixed by category? Who contracts with consumer and who bears statutory seller/provider duties? |
| Consumer protection | Required seller/price/fee disclosures, distance contract, pre-contract information, complaint/return/refund rights, language requirements and records? [Consumer Protection Law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/Z100000274_) |
| Payment services | Does the chosen funds flow make KUNDE a payment-service provider, agent/subagent, payment aggregator or other regulated participant? Which authorizations/registrations/contracts apply? [Law on Payments and Payment Systems](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/Z1600000011) |
| Tax/VAT/accounting | Principal vs agent recognition, commission VAT, tax invoices, platform reporting, withholding, provider/merchant settlements, promo subsidy and refund accounting under current Tax Code? [Tax Code of Kazakhstan](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214) |
| Personal data | Controller/operator roles, consent/other basis, local storage, cross-border processor, child/health/location data, deletion/retention, breach notification? [Personal Data Law](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/Z1300000094), [collection/processing rules](https://old.adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/V2000021498) |
| Online platform/ads | Platform duties, advertising labeling, sponsored ranking, user content, takedown/complaints and marketplace transparency? [Law on Online Platforms and Online Advertising](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2300000018) |
| Partners/providers | Entity/individual entrepreneur verification, contract terms, licenses, insurance, liability, termination, dispute and classification as employee/contractor? |
| Regulated categories | Health/clinic/pharmacy, transport, education/children, financial services, food safety, tickets/consumer warranties; licenses, advertising and data controls? |
| Refund/dispute | Mandatory rights and deadlines by category, allocation across platform/merchant/processor, chargeback evidence and complaint escalation? |
| Marketing/reviews | Consent to marketing, referral programs, review moderation, fake review penalties, consumer claim substantiation and ad disclosures? |

## Payment law caution

Adilet’s updated English translation of the Payments Law describes regulated payment services and authorization/registration requirements; it also lists exclusions/roles whose applicability depends on actual facts. The document must not be used to conclude that KUNDE needs—or does not need—a particular license. Legal counsel should review the final flow diagram, contract and licensed provider status, including amendments effective by launch date.

## Launch legal checklist

Written marketplace/payment role memo; consumer and partner terms; category-specific cancellation/refund disclosures; privacy notice and consent records; vendor/data-processing and residency assessment; partner/provider verification matrix; tax/accounting opinion; advertising/sponsored listing rules; regulated-category approval; records-retention schedule; complaint/incident response process; translated Kazakh/Russian legal copy review. Do not activate category/payment model until approved owners sign off.
