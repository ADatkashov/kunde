# 20 — International Expansion

No country is selected. Expansion requires country-specific legal, tax, payment, data and operating diligence; Kazakhstan assumptions do not transfer automatically.

## Global capabilities vs local requirements

| Global platform capability | Country/city-specific decision |
|---|---|
| Identity, roles, catalog, search, order/booking/payment state machines | Identity assurance, consumer contract language, age rules, prohibited categories |
| Multi-currency amounts and locale formatting | Currency, tax/VAT, invoicing, accounting recognition, commission/tax treatment |
| Region/country partition identifiers | Data residency, transfer basis, processor/subprocessor, retention and regulator reporting |
| Payment provider abstraction | Licensed provider, acquirer methods, settlement, chargeback, payment agent/intermediary limits |
| Address/time-zone abstraction | Address formats, maps coverage, local time zones and service-area geometry |
| Partner/provider roles | Local entity/contract, employment classification, insurance, permits and safety rules |
| Translation resources and support tooling | Languages, required disclosures, customer service hours and escalation |

## Market entry gates

Country legal entity/contracting model; local counsel opinion for marketplace/payment role; tax/accounting memo; licensed payment provider and settlement test; data-region/transfer assessment; consumer rights/refund terms; partner/provider classification; category licensing; local content/ad rules; support hiring/language; logistics capacity; partner supply economics; local incident/cyber obligations; translated UX/legal review. Market lead must demonstrate sustainable supply and contribution before broad acquisition.

## Operating model

Use regional data cell when law or latency requires; country policy/config controls enabled categories and commercial terms. Avoid global PII replication. Global analytics uses approved aggregation/pseudonymization only. Determine local support, trust, finance and compliance owners before launch. Country pilot follows same staged Go/No-Go method as Almaty, with local metrics and rollback.
