# 21 — Business Risk Register

Probability/impact are initial qualitative hypotheses, not measured likelihood. Owner names are roles to assign. Severity: Critical/High/Medium.

| Risk | Probability | Impact / severity | Early warning indicators | Mitigation | Owner | Contingency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Insufficient partner supply | High | High | Empty search, low available hours, merchant response drop | Narrow zones/categories, recruit and verify before promotion | Partnerships | Restrict service area; pause category |
| Insufficient demand/low frequency | Medium-high | High | Low search conversion/repeat | User research, channel tests, retention cohorts | Product/Growth | Reposition or stop category investment |
| High CAC | Medium-high | High | CAC > cohort contribution | Capped experiments, partner channels, attribution | Growth/Finance | Pause paid media; focus retention/supply |
| Low contribution margin | High | Critical | Negative contribution after delivery/support/promo | Per-order cost tracking and pricing tests | Finance/Product | Remove subsidy/lane or do not scale |
| Payment-provider dependency/outage | Medium | High | Unknown payment aging, auth failures | Reconciliation, provider SLA, fallback policy | Finance/Engineering | Pause new paid transactions; resolve in-flight |
| OTP/maps cost/dependency | Medium | Medium | Cost/request spikes, provider outage | Rate limits, manual fallbacks, adapter review | Engineering/Finance | Throttle noncritical use; alternate provider after approval |
| Delivery cost/service failure | High | High | Cost per order, late/fail rate increase | Partner delivery pilot, zone caps, clear ETA | Operations | Switch to pickup/disable delivery zone |
| Fraud/refund abuse | Medium | High | Clustered accounts/refunds/chargebacks | Risk signals, human review, policy and evidence | Trust/Finance | Restrict suspicious flow; preserve legitimate appeal |
| Partner/provider quality | Medium-high | High | Acceptance/completion decline, complaints | Verification, scorecards, coaching and suspension path | Operations | Pause/replace supply, support affected consumers |
| Consumer disputes/regulatory role unclear | Medium | Critical | Complaints, counsel questions, contract ambiguity | Legal classification and transparent terms before launch | Compliance/Legal | Stop affected payment/category model |
| Data/privacy incident | Low-medium | Critical | Access anomaly, misrouted data, vendor log leakage | Least privilege, data minimization, residency review | Security/Privacy | Incident response, contain, notify as required |
| Operational overload | Medium | High | Backlog, SLA miss, unresolved payment exceptions | Volume cap, staffing forecast, kill switches | Operations | Limit hours/zone/acquisition |
| AI action/claim risk | Medium | High | Unauthorized tool, false completion or biased output | Confirmation, tool policy, human review and eval | AI/Product/Security | Disable affected tools/model, manual support |
| Expansion overreach | Medium | High | New city lacks support/supply economics | Scorecard and one-city-at-a-time launch | Executive/Market lead | Halt city activation and close open cases |

Review monthly and during launch incident; assign accountable person and quantitative triggers before pilot.
