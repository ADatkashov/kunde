# 04 — Partner Business Model

KUNDE should distinguish merchant/business partners from individual providers and logistics partners. Contracting entity, seller of record, service provider and payment beneficiary can differ; each must be disclosed and legally validated per category.

| Partner type | Value proposition | Onboarding/operating needs | Candidate commercial model / responsibilities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant/cafe | Incremental local discovery/orders, menu tools, status/feedback | Business verification, location/menu/hours, fulfillment model, training | Transaction commission hypothesis; partner owns accurate menu, food prep, acceptance, packaging and disclosed allergen data |
| Grocery/supermarket | Digital catalog, local demand, repeat basket | Stock freshness, substitution/returns process, delivery/pickup and branch operations | Commission, SaaS or fulfillment fee; partner owns inventory/quality and item legality |
| Retail store | Searchable local inventory and online orders | Product attributes, stock, brand rights, returns/warranty | Category fee/lead/subscription; partner owns product accuracy, warranty and lawful sale |
| Clinic/health provider | Discovery and appointment management | Licensing/credentials, regulated service review, patient privacy | No transaction monetization until counsel confirms; provider owns clinical service and professional decisions |
| Salon/personal care | Schedule utilization and new customer bookings | Staff/resources, slot controls, cancellation rules | Booking commission or SaaS; provider owns service quality and credentials |
| Service company (home/auto) | Qualified demand, quotes, schedule, case support | Scope/quote, technician eligibility, insurance as required | Lead or completed-job commission; partner/provider owns agreed scope, workmanship and job safety |
| Education/children’s center | Enrollment discovery and schedule coordination | Program, capacity, instructor/guardian controls, safeguards | Enrollment/seat fee or tools; institution responsible for instruction/safeguarding under contract/law |
| Event organizer/venue | Ticket reach, inventory and attendee updates | Inventory, fees, ticket validation, event cancellation | Ticket fee/commission; organizer owns event/admission, KUNDE routes reliable information |
| Sole proprietor/small business | Low-friction digital storefront and booking/order access | Simple verification, owner account, catalog and bank verification | Simplified transparent commission/tools; no waiver of legal verification |
| Enterprise/multi-branch | Central catalog, locations, roles, analytics/integration | API/data governance, service standards, reconciliation and support contacts | Negotiated volume/SaaS/API contract; enterprise must scope grants and data responsibility |
| Logistics/last-mile partner | Access to eligible delivery demand | Coverage, insurance/driver verification, rate cards, proof/status events | Per-task/service contract; partner responsible for promised logistics performance |

## Contractual responsibilities

Partner must provide truthful business/legal/contact data, maintain authorization/licences, publish accurate prices/availability, accept/reject in agreed windows, fulfill accepted scope, handle required warranty/consumer obligations, cooperate on evidence/refunds, protect consumer data, train staff, report incidents, and honor audit/termination terms. KUNDE provides agreed platform access, discovery/order routing, clear fee disclosure, status/support and settlement reporting. Neither party’s obligations are assumed beyond executed contract and law.

## Suspension/termination

Grounds can include expired verification, unlawful/prohibited offer, repeated fulfillment failure, fraud, safety/privacy breach, payment/settlement irregularity or contract breach. Apply proportionate restriction, evidence, notice and remediation/appeal unless urgent safety/legal risk requires immediate hold. Existing orders/bookings and customer remedies remain managed through continuity plan; do not strand paid consumers.
