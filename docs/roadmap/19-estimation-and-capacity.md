# 19 — Estimation & Capacity Planning

No calendar dates, velocity, story points or headcount-based delivery duration are asserted: team composition, availability, vendor lead times and decision turnaround are unknown.

Use T-shirt sizing during refinement: S = isolated low-risk change; M = bounded feature across a module; L = cross-module or external contract; XL = split before commitment. Maintain separate dimensions: engineering complexity, operational/legal risk, and external dependency confidence. A small task can still be blocked by a high-risk legal/vendor dependency.

After a staffed team completes representative work, establish observed cycle time/throughput and estimate a release range with explicit assumptions, contingency and confidence. Best case assumes decisions/vendors are ready and no major rework; expected case includes normal integration/review; risk-adjusted case includes vendor/legal delay, migration/recovery work and scope cuts. Do not convert these scenarios into calendar dates until owners provide capacity and evidence.

Initial sequencing is decision closure and foundation; no duration estimate is required to identify the critical path. Report capacity by accountable role and work-in-progress limits, not aggregate person-weeks alone.
