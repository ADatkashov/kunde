# 21 — Operational Readiness

## Technical readiness

Production-like staging; deploy/rollback; approved SLO/RPO/RTO (D-12); alerts with owners; encrypted backup and witnessed restore; tested integration timeout/replay/reconciliation; on-call rota; category/zone pause control; incident severity/escalation and customer-impact communication; privacy/security sign-off.

## Business readiness

One approved category/zone/hours; active, verified partners and accurate catalog; written fulfillment responsibility and capacity; partner/provider onboarding and escalation; customer support staffing and scripts; approved terms, fee/price display and cancellation/refund policy; payment operations and daily reconciliation; dispute ownership; signed partner agreements; finance/legal/trust approvals; customer help content and language review.

## Rehearsals and go/no-go

Run tabletop and test transactions for partner rejection, no response, unavailable item, late/failed fulfillment, payment unknown/success/decline, duplicate callback, cancel, refund, charge dispute, support escalation, incident/pause and recovery. Verify order/payment/provider records reconcile and customer communication is accurate. Do not launch if critical issue is unowned, support coverage is absent, payment status is ambiguous, provider responsibility is unsigned, or policy/legal approval is missing. Exact volume thresholds are set from pilot evidence, not fabricated here.
